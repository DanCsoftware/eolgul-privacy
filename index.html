THE SHORT VERSION

Your scan photos are sent to Anthropic, an AI company, so their Claude model can analyze your skin. That is the core of how Eolgul works, and we ask your permission in the app before it ever happens.

Nobody trains AI on your photos. We do not sell your data, show you ads, or track you across other apps.

Who we are

Eolgul Labs ("we," "us") provides a cosmetic skincare guidance app for iOS. This policy explains what we collect, how we collect it, every use we make of it, and who else receives it.

What we collect and why

Sharing your photos with Anthropic

Eolgul cannot analyze your skin without an AI model, and we do not run one ourselves. When you scan, your three photos and the skin concerns you selected are sent over an encrypted connection to Anthropic PBC and processed by their Claude API.

What Anthropic receives

Your three scan photos, the skin concerns you selected, and, on repeat scans, the photos from your most recent previous scan so the model can compare them. On product lookups, the product photo you took.

What Anthropic does not receive

Your name, your email, your account identifier, and your device identifier are never sent. Anthropic receives images and skin concern labels with no information about who you are.

What Anthropic does with it

Anthropic processes the request and returns the analysis to our server. Under Anthropic's Commercial Terms, which govern our use of their API, inputs and outputs from the API are not used to train their models. Anthropic retains API inputs and outputs for a limited period for operational and safety purposes and then deletes them. Anthropic's own privacy documentation is available at privacy.claude.com.

Equal protection

Anthropic processes this data on our behalf as a service provider, under contractual terms that prohibit using it for their own purposes and that provide protections equivalent to those described in this policy. We do not grant them the right to sell it, advertise from it, or train on it.

Your permission

Before your first scan, Eolgul shows you a screen explaining that your photos will be sent to Anthropic and asks you to agree. If you decline, no photo is ever transmitted and no scan takes place. You can withdraw by deleting your account at any time.

Where your data is stored

Photos, scan results, and routines are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage), encrypted at rest and scoped to your account. Analysis runs on Google Cloud Functions. Data is processed in the United States.

What we never do

  • We never sell your photos or your data.
  • We never share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or affiliates.
  • We never use your photos to train AI models, ours or anyone else's, and neither does Anthropic.
  • We include no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking software of any kind.
  • We never derive facial recognition or biometric identity templates from your photos. We analyze visible skin characteristics such as texture, tone, and clarity. We do not use your photos to identify you as a person.
  • We do not view your photos as a company unless you specifically ask us to for support.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your browsing history, your microphone, your other apps, or your activity outside Eolgul. Photos are stripped of camera metadata when they are processed on your device.

Your control and your rights

You can delete your account and its data from Settings inside the app, or by emailing us. When you delete your account we remove your photos, scan history, and routines. Deletion begins immediately and completes within 30 days. We keep no shadow copies.

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK or the CCPA in California. Exercise them in the app or by emailing us.

Children

Eolgul is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it. Where local law sets a higher age of digital consent, that age applies.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. If we change what is sent to Anthropic, we will ask for your permission again. We will not change our retention or use commitments retroactively without giving you the chance to delete your data first.

Contact

Questions about your privacy or this policy? Email eolgulLabs@proton.me. We read every message and reply within 2 business days.
