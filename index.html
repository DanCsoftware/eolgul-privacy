THE SHORT VERSION

Your face photos never leave your phone. Your phone measures your skin itself, and only those measurements (numbers, not pictures) and your answers are sent to Anthropic's Claude to write your results, build your routine and grade it. The app asks your permission before anything is sent, and works without AI if you say no.

We ask your age range first. If you're under 18, nothing about you is sent to Anthropic and the app doesn't scan your face. If you're under 13, Eolgul is a checklist that stays on your phone: nothing about you is sent to us at all.

Nobody trains AI on your data. We don't sell your data, show you ads, or track you across other apps.

Who we are

Eolgul Labs ("we," "us") provides a cosmetic skincare guidance app for iOS. This policy explains what we collect, how we collect it, every use we make of it, and who else receives it.

What we collect and why

  • Your age range. For example 25–34, asked when you start. Used to choose products and how strong they are, and sent to Anthropic with your answers when your routine is built or graded. Under 18, it turns off scanning and the AI features. Under 13, it isn't sent to us at all (see "If you're under 13").
  • Face photos (3 per scan). Taken with your camera after you allow it. Measured on your phone and kept there so you can look back at them; never uploaded to our servers or sent to Anthropic. You choose how long they're kept in Settings, and your first scan's photos are kept until you delete them. See "Face data" below.
  • Skin measurements. Numbers such as redness, tone and brightness per area of the face, calculated on your phone. Used for your scores, sent to Anthropic to write your results, and saved to your account and our measurement records (see "Improving the measurement").
  • Your answers about your skin. Skin type, goals, and how your skin reacts to products, from setup or Settings under Your skin. Used to build and grade your routine and sent to Anthropic with your measurements.
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding status (optional). Used to leave out products with ingredients commonly avoided during pregnancy and breastfeeding, and sent to Anthropic so the written routine respects it.
  • Allergies and sensitivities (optional). Matched to the names ingredients are listed under, so products containing them are left out or flagged. Wording we don't recognise is sent to Anthropic to work out the ingredient names (never for under-18s).
  • Your routine, the steps you check off, and its grades. Shown to you, tracked over time, and used for your progress calendar. When you ask for a grade, your routine's products and your answers are sent to Anthropic.
  • Product photos (optional). Only if you use Capture my routine or Scan the bottle. Sent to Anthropic to read the product's name and ingredients. We keep the product we found, not the photo.
  • Product names you type (optional). If a product isn't in our list and you choose Look it up, the name you typed is sent to Anthropic to find it.
  • iPhone model. Recorded with each scan, to check that different iPhones read the same skin the same way.
  • Error reports. What went wrong, where in the app, and your app and iOS version. Never your photos, scores, routine or anything you typed.
  • Name and email. Only if you create an account with Sign in with Apple, to identify your account and restore your data on a new device. Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real email.
  • An account identifier. Created by Firebase after you tell us you're 13 or older, to keep your data separate from everyone else's. Used only to run the app, never to contact or profile you, and never sent to Anthropic. Under 13, none is created.

Face data

What face data the app collects

Eolgul takes three photos of your face for each scan: one from the front and one of each cheek. While you line up a photo, the phone finds your face in the camera view to check framing and angle, and in each photo it finds your eyes, nose and mouth so it knows where your forehead, nose, chin and cheeks are. This face detection uses Apple's and Google's on-device frameworks and runs entirely on your phone; the images are not sent to Apple or Google. From the five skin areas it then calculates skin measurements: numbers such as redness, tone, brightness and surface texture for each area.

How it is used

The face positions are used only on your phone, to place and check the five measurement areas in each photo. They are not saved to our servers, not sent to Anthropic or anyone else, and never used to recognise you, to identify you, or to create a facial-recognition or biometric template. The skin measurements produce your scores, are compared with your own earlier scans to show change, and are used to write your results and build your routine.

Where it is stored and how long it is kept

Face photos are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to our servers and never sent to Anthropic or any other third party. They are kept for the period you choose in Settings (30 days, 90 days, 1 year, or until you delete them; 1 year unless you change it), except your first scan's photos, which are kept as your starting point until you delete them. You can delete the photos from any scan at any time, and deleting your account or the app removes all of them.

Only the skin measurements leave your phone. They contain no images and no face positions, and cannot be turned back into a picture of your face. They are stored with Google Firebase in the United States, linked to your account, and kept until you delete your account, when they are removed within 30 days.

Sharing

Face photos and face positions are not shared with anyone. The skin measurements are sent to Anthropic, which writes your results (see "Sharing with Anthropic" below); Anthropic does not use them for training and deletes them within 30 days. We do not sell face data or share it with advertisers, data brokers or anyone else.

Sharing with Anthropic

Eolgul's written results, routines, grades and product look-ups are produced by an AI model, and we do not run one ourselves. They are produced by the Claude API of Anthropic PBC, over an encrypted connection.

What Anthropic receives

Your skin measurements, your age range, your answers, your pregnancy or breastfeeding status, your allergies, your routine's products when you ask for a grade, and product photos or names you choose to look up.

What Anthropic does not receive

Your face photos, your name, your email, your account identifier, and your device identifier are never sent.

What Anthropic does with it

Under Anthropic's commercial terms, which govern our use of their API, what we send is not used to train their models and is deleted within 30 days. Anthropic processes it on our behalf only, as a service provider; we don't give them the right to sell it, advertise from it, or train on it. Anthropic's own privacy documentation is at privacy.claude.com.

Your permission

Before anything is sent, Eolgul shows you what is sent and asks you to allow or decline. If you decline, nothing is sent: your routine is built from your answers using our own fixed rules, and you can allow AI later in Settings. The same explanation is in Settings under How Eolgul uses AI.

Your health information

Your answers about your skin, your pregnancy or breastfeeding status, your allergies and your skin measurements are health information. Eolgul asks your permission before storing any of it, separately from the AI permission. If you say no, nothing about your skin or health is stored and you still get a basic routine. You can stop at any time in Settings under Your health information. We then delete it from our servers within 30 days and from any backups within 6 months; your routine stays. Our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy explains this in full.

If you're 13 to 17

Eolgul doesn't scan your face, doesn't send anything about you to Anthropic, and doesn't grade your routine. You build your own routine from our product list, and we point out products that may be too strong for your age. Your age range, the things you avoid and your routine are stored with your account like anyone else's, so you can delete them the same way.

If you're under 13

Eolgul works as a checklist that stays on your phone. You add the products you already use, tick them off, and the app checks them for things to look at. The product list and the checks are inside the app, so nothing about you is sent to us, to Anthropic, or to anyone else, and there is no account. There is no account identifier either: Eolgul creates one only after you tell us you're 13 or older. On a phone that had one from before, it is deleted when the checklist starts. Once "under 13" is chosen on a phone, Eolgul remembers it there, so the app stays a checklist on that phone. Your checklist isn't backed up: deleting the app or changing phones deletes it, and Delete my checklist in Settings removes it at once.

If an existing account changes its age range to under 13, its routine, ticks and the things it avoids move to the phone, and the account and everything stored with it on our servers is deleted.

Where your data is stored

Your scores, answers, routines and account are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), encrypted at rest and scoped to your account. Analysis runs on Google Cloud Functions. Data is processed in the United States. Face photos and under-13 checklists are not stored on our servers.

Improving the measurement

When you scan, we also keep the numbers the app measured (readings such as redness, tone, brightness and surface texture, per facial zone) in a separate set of measurement records. These records contain the numbers and a reference to the account they came from, so we can delete them when you delete your account. They never contain your photos, your name, your email, or anything you typed.

We keep them because a skin scanner that cannot tell people apart would look, from the inside, exactly like one that works. Comparing readings across many different faces is the only way to know the difference, and it is what lets us correct the measurement when it is wrong.

What we never do

  • We never upload your face photos.
  • We never sell your data.
  • We never share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or affiliates.
  • We never use your data to train AI models, ours or anyone else's, and neither does Anthropic.
  • We include no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking software. We do not use a crash-reporting service.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your browsing history, your microphone, your other apps, or your activity outside Eolgul.

Your control and your rights

Face photos on your phone can be deleted from any scan, or cleared automatically with the setting in Settings. You can delete your account from Settings inside the app, or by emailing us. Deleting your account removes the photos on your phone at once, and your scores, answers, routines and measurement records from our servers within 30 days. We keep no shadow copies.

You can use Eolgul before creating an account. What you enter is still stored, under an anonymous account. To delete it, create an account and use Delete account, or email us.

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK or the CCPA in California. Exercise them in the app or by emailing us.

Children

Eolgul doesn't collect personal data from children under 13. Under 13, the app is a checklist that stays on the phone (see "If you're under 13"). If you believe a child has given us data anyway, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. If we change what is sent to Anthropic, we will ask for your permission again. We will not change our retention or use commitments retroactively without giving you the chance to delete your data first.
