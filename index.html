THE SHORT VERSION

Your face photos never leave your phone. Your phone measures your skin itself, and only those measurements (numbers, not pictures) and your answers are sent to Anthropic's Claude to write your results and build your routine. The app asks your permission before anything is sent.

Nobody trains AI on your data. We do not sell your data, show you ads, or track you across other apps.

Who we are

Eolgul Labs ("we," "us") provides a cosmetic skincare guidance app for iOS. This policy explains what we collect, how we collect it, every use we make of it, and who else receives it.

What happens on your phone

The scan itself runs on your phone. Eolgul measures your photos locally (colour, brightness and surface detail across five facial zones) and those measurements produce your scores. The photos are never uploaded. We do not derive facial-recognition or biometric identity templates, and we do not use any of this to identify you as a person.

Sharing with Anthropic

Eolgul's written results and routines are produced by an AI model, and we do not run one ourselves. When you scan, your measurements and your answers are sent over an encrypted connection to Anthropic PBC and processed by their Claude API.

What Anthropic receives

Your skin measurements (numbers), your answers about your skin, your pregnancy or breastfeeding status, and your allergies. On product lookups, the product photo you took. When you describe an allergy in words we don't recognise, those words.

What Anthropic does not receive

Your face photos, your name, your email, your account identifier, and your device identifier are never sent.

What Anthropic does with it

Anthropic processes the request and returns the result to our server. Under Anthropic's Commercial Terms, which govern our use of their API, inputs and outputs are not used to train their models, and Anthropic deletes them within 30 days. Anthropic's own privacy documentation is available at privacy.claude.com.

Equal protection

Anthropic processes this data on our behalf as a service provider, under contractual terms that prohibit using it for their own purposes. We do not grant them the right to sell it, advertise from it, or train on it.

Your permission

Before your first scan, Eolgul shows you a screen that says what is sent, who receives it, and what they do with it, and asks you to allow or decline. If you decline, nothing is sent and no scan takes place. The same explanation is available any time in Settings under "How Eolgul uses AI". You can withdraw by deleting your account at any time.

Where your data is stored

Your scores, answers, routines and account are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), encrypted at rest and scoped to your account. Analysis runs on Google Cloud Functions. Data is processed in the United States. Face photos are not stored on our servers.

Improving the measurement

When you scan, we also keep the numbers the app measured (readings such as redness, tone, brightness and surface texture, per facial zone) in a separate set of measurement records. These records contain the numbers and a reference to the account they came from, so we can delete them when you delete your account. They never contain your photos, your name, your email, or anything you typed.

We keep them because a skin scanner that cannot tell people apart would look, from the inside, exactly like one that works. Comparing readings across many different faces is the only way to know the difference, and it is what lets us correct the measurement when it is wrong.

What we never do

  • We never upload your face photos.
  • We never sell your data.
  • We never share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or affiliates.
  • We never use your data to train AI models, ours or anyone else's, and neither does Anthropic.
  • We include no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking software. We do not use a crash-reporting service.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your browsing history, your microphone, your other apps, or your activity outside Eolgul.

Your control and your rights

Face photos on your phone can be deleted from any scan, or cleared automatically with the setting in Settings. You can delete your account from Settings inside the app, or by emailing us. Deleting your account removes the photos on your phone at once, and your scores, answers, routines and measurement records from our servers within 30 days. We keep no shadow copies.

You can use Eolgul before creating an account. What you enter is still stored, under an anonymous account. To delete it, create an account and use Delete account, or email us.

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK or the CCPA in California. Exercise them in the app or by emailing us.

Children

Eolgul is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it. Where local law sets a higher age of digital consent, that age applies.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. If we change what is sent to Anthropic, we will ask for your permission again. We will not change our retention or use commitments retroactively without giving you the chance to delete your data first.
