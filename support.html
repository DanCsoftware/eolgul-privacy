Getting started

What Eolgul does

Eolgul takes three photos of your face, scores five zones from 0 to 100, identifies the concerns it sees, and builds a K-beauty routine for morning and evening based on what your skin actually shows.

Do I need an account?

No. You can complete a full scan and see your results and routine without signing in. Creating an account lets you keep your history across scans and restore it if you change devices. Sign in with Apple is supported.

How to take a good scan

Face a window or a lamp so the light comes from in front of you, not behind.

Hold the phone at eye level, roughly an arm's length away.

Remove makeup first. Eolgul reads your skin, not your foundation.

Tie hair back so your forehead and cheeks are visible.

Scan at a consistent time of day. Comparing a morning scan to a late-night one adds variation that has nothing to do with your skin.

Troubleshooting

The camera will not take my photo

Eolgul checks lighting and face position before every shot and holds the capture until both are good. This is deliberate. A photo taken in poor light produces an unreliable score. Move toward brighter, front-facing light, center your face in the guide, and hold steady. The shutter releases on its own once conditions pass.

Analysis failed or timed out

Analysis runs on our servers and usually takes 10 to 20 seconds. If it fails, check your connection and try again. A failed scan does not count against your daily limit. If it fails repeatedly, email us with the approximate time it happened so we can look at the logs.

It says I have reached my scan limit

Scans are limited to 3 per day per account. Skin does not change meaningfully within hours, so more frequent scanning mostly measures lighting rather than progress. The limit resets on a rolling daily basis.

The camera permission prompt never appeared

Open the iOS Settings app, scroll to Eolgul, and enable Camera. If you previously declined, iOS will not ask again from inside the app.

My routine did not generate

Open Settings inside the app and tap Regenerate my routine. This rebuilds your routine from your most recent scan. If it still comes back empty, email us.

Sign in with Apple asks me twice

A known issue we are working on. Completing the second prompt signs you in normally and your data is unaffected.

Understanding your results

The five zones

Forehead, T-zone, left cheek, right cheek, and chin. Skin rarely behaves the same way across the whole face, so a single overall number hides more than it tells you. Tap any zone on the face map to see what Eolgul saw there and what it recommends.

Why my score barely moved

Eolgul hides changes smaller than 3 points. Repeated scans of the same skin on the same day vary by about that much purely from lighting and angle, so a shift of 1 or 2 points is measurement noise rather than a real result. Showing it would be flattering and dishonest. Changes of 3 points or more are real enough to display.

Why my whole face scored higher today

If every zone moved up or down together, that is usually the room rather than your skin. Brighter, more even light lifts scores across the board. Scanning in a consistent spot makes your history far more useful.

Scores are whole numbers

Deliberately. Decimal places would imply a precision the measurement does not have.

Changing your concerns

The concerns Eolgul tracks for you can be edited. Open your concerns list, tap any concern to read what it is and what helps, and add or remove it. Your routine updates to match.

Your routine

Where the recommendations come from

Products are drawn from a curated K-beauty catalog and matched to the concerns detected in your scan. Every step explains why it is there, what to expect from it, and how to apply it. Tap any step to read the full detail.

Pregnancy and nursing

Eolgul includes ingredient safety filtering for pregnancy and nursing. You can turn this on during onboarding or later in Settings. When it is on, ingredients commonly avoided during pregnancy are excluded from your recommendations. This is a convenience filter and not medical clearance. Talk to your doctor or midwife about anything you put on your skin while pregnant or nursing.

Can I use my own products instead?

Yes. The routine is guidance, not a shopping list. The reasoning attached to each step tells you what job that product is doing, so you can substitute something you already own that does the same job.
