Common questions

The camera will not take my photo.
Eolgul checks lighting and framing before each shot. Move to brighter, front-facing light and hold the phone at eye level. The capture releases once conditions are good.

My score changed by only a point or two.
Changes smaller than 3 points fall within measurement noise, so Eolgul hides them rather than showing them as real progress.

How many scans can I do?
Three per day.

How do I delete my data?
Email the address above and your account and stored photos will be removed.
