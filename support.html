Getting started

What Eolgul does

Eolgul takes three photos of your face, measures redness and dark spots across five areas of your face on your phone, and builds a morning and evening Korean skincare routine from your skin type, your goals, how your skin reacts to products, whether you're pregnant or breastfeeding, and anything you're allergic to.

Do I need an account?

Not to scan. You can answer the setup questions, take a scan and see your results as a guest. Your routine needs a free account, created with Sign in with Apple, because it's saved to your account and tracked over the days you use it. An account also restores your history if you change phones. There is nothing to buy.

Your photos and AI

Your face photos never leave your phone. The phone measures your skin itself, and only those measurements (numbers such as redness and brightness for each area, never images) and your setup answers are sent to Anthropic's Claude, which writes your results and builds your routine. Your name, email and account identifier are never sent.

Before your first scan the app asks your permission and says exactly what is sent. If you decline, nothing is sent and no scan takes place; the rest of the app still works. The same explanation is in Settings under "How Eolgul uses AI", and the full detail, including a section on face data, is in the Privacy Policy.

How to take a good scan

Face a window or a lamp so the light comes from in front of you, not behind.

Hold the phone at eye level, roughly an arm's length away.

Keep a neutral expression.

Remove makeup first. Eolgul reads your skin, not your foundation.

Tie hair back so your forehead and cheeks are visible.

Scan in the same spot at a similar time of day. Different light is the biggest cause of a reading that moves when your skin hasn't.

Troubleshooting

The camera will not take my photo

Eolgul checks lighting and face position before every shot and holds the capture until both are good. This is deliberate: a photo taken in poor light gives an unreliable reading. Move toward brighter, front-facing light, center your face in the guide, and hold steady. The shutter releases on its own once the checks pass.

My scan was rejected after the photo was taken

Occasionally a photo passes the on-screen checks and still turns out too dark, too bright or too uneven to measure. Eolgul tells you why and asks for another rather than giving a score it can't stand behind. A rejected capture doesn't count against your daily scan.

Analysis failed or timed out

Analysis usually takes 10 to 20 seconds. Your first scan takes longer because it also builds your routine, which can take up to a minute; the screen shows each step as it finishes. If analysis fails, check your connection and try again. A failed scan doesn't count against your daily scan. If it keeps failing, email us with the approximate time so we can look into it.

It says I've already scanned today

Eolgul allows one scan a day. Skin doesn't change meaningfully within hours, so scanning more often mostly measures the light in the room. The limit resets once a day at midnight UTC, which is late afternoon in the United States.

The camera permission prompt never appeared

If you declined camera access before, iOS won't ask again from inside the app. Tap "Open Settings" on the scan screen, or open the iOS Settings app, scroll to Eolgul, and turn on Camera.

My routine did not generate

The building screen tells you if your routine didn't build. Open Settings inside the app and tap "Regenerate my routine", which rebuilds it from your latest scan. If it still comes back empty, email us.

Deleting my account opened a Sign in with Apple screen

That's expected. Apple asks you to confirm once so Eolgul can be removed from your Sign in with Apple list as part of the deletion. If you close that screen, nothing is deleted.

Understanding your results

The five areas

Forehead, nose, chin, left cheek and right cheek. Skin rarely behaves the same way across the whole face, so a single overall number hides more than it tells you. Tap any area on the face map to see its readings.

What the scan measures

Redness and dark spots, scored in each area and compared with your own earlier scans, never with anyone else's face. A dark-circle reading is shown, but it isn't compared or counted yet, because it doesn't hold steady enough under different light. The scan doesn't measure oiliness, pores, fine lines, texture or dullness. If you tell us about those, your routine still takes them into account.

Why my score barely moved

Eolgul only calls a change a direction when it's bigger than the scan's own margin of error, and only once you have scans on at least three different days. Repeated scans of the same skin vary a little from light and angle alone, so a small change is shown but not called progress. If your skin hasn't changed, Eolgul says so.

Why my whole face scored differently today

If every area moved up or down together, that's usually the room rather than your skin. Brighter, more even light shifts readings across the board. Scanning in a consistent spot makes your history far more useful.

Changing what you told us

Open Settings, then "Your skin", to change your skin type answers, goals and reactions. Pregnancy and breastfeeding, and allergies and sensitivities, have their own rows in Settings.

Your routine

Where the recommendations come from

Products come from a curated Korean skincare catalog. Your routine is chosen for your goals and skin type, with anything matching your allergies left out, and every step explains what the product does, why it was picked for you and how to use it. Products are named only; Eolgul doesn't sell them or link to stores.

Pregnancy and breastfeeding

If you tell us you're pregnant or breastfeeding, products containing retinoids, hydroquinone, arbutin, strong salicylic acid or chemical sunscreen filters are left out of your routine. This is a filter, not medical clearance. Talk to your doctor or midwife about anything you put on your skin while pregnant or breastfeeding.

Allergies

Describe your allergies in your own words. Eolgul matches them to the names ingredients are listed under and leaves out every product that contains them, along with any product whose full ingredient list we couldn't check.

Can I use my own products instead?

Yes. "Capture my routine" lets you photograph products you already own, and Eolgul grades the routine you use and suggests changes where something doesn't suit you. Product photos are sent off the phone so the product can be identified. Face photos never are.

Account and data

What we store

Your scan scores and skin measurements (numbers, never images), your setup answers, your routine and the steps you check off, your iPhone model, and your name and email if you signed in with Apple. Face photos stay on your phone, kept for the period you choose in Settings under "Keep scan photos".

Deleting your account and data

Open Settings inside the app and tap Delete account. This removes the photos and data on your phone at once, and your scores, answers, routine and measurement records from our servers. You can also email us from the address on your account if you'd rather we did it, and we'll confirm once it's done. If you never created an account, delete the app and email us to remove what was stored for your guest session.

Email us and we'll send you what we hold on your account.
