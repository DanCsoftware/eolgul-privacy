Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11 · Last updated November 2026

This policy covers the health information Eolgul stores, as required by Washington's My Health My Data Act. It sits alongside our Privacy Policy, which covers everything else.

What health data we collect

Your skin type, goals and how your skin reacts; whether you're pregnant or breastfeeding, if you tell us; allergies and ingredients you avoid; and, if you scan, the skin measurements your phone calculates (never the photos).

Where it comes from

From you, when you answer our questions, and from your phone, which calculates the skin measurements from your scan. We don't get health data about you from anyone else.

Why

Only to build your routine, check it against what you avoid, grade it if you ask, and show your progress. Never for ads, and never sold.

Who receives it

Google Firebase stores it for us, encrypted, in the United States. Anthropic's Claude receives it when it writes your results or routine, only if you're 18 or over and have allowed AI; Anthropic doesn't train on it and deletes it within 30 days. No one else.

Your consent

We ask before storing any of it. You can say no and still get a basic routine. You can stop at any time in Settings, under Your health information.

Stopping and deleting

When you stop, we delete your skin answers, pregnancy answer, allergies and scan measurements from our servers within 30 days, and from any backups within 6 months. Firebase holds it for us, so deleting it there deletes it. What Anthropic already received is deleted by Anthropic within 30 days; we can't recall it sooner. Your routine stays. You can also delete your whole account in Settings.

Your rights

You can stop and delete it yourself in Settings. To ask what we hold, or for anything the app can't do, email daniel_h_cha@proton.me. We reply within 45 days, usually much sooner.

Appeals

If we turn down a request, we'll tell you why. You can appeal by replying to that email, and we'll answer within 45 days. If you're still not satisfied, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General.

Under 13

Nothing about a child under 13 is stored by us. Their checklist stays on their phone.
